About

I was born in the US, then moved to Tokyo, Hong Kong, and back to the US, while visiting my grandparents in Shanghai every year. Without realizing it, my upbringing has drawn me to building bridges between worlds. As a TA in undergrad, I loved helping psych and gov majors apply the CS concepts they learned to their respective fields. As a PM at Microsoft, I loved serving as the connector across roles and geographies.

Currently I'm a student at HBS, where I lead the AI Club and host AI Workshops for folks with no background knowledge. In my spare time, I've been building a language learning app, starting with Cantonese! Otherwise, you'll find me on a basketball court, or watching the Clippers and Hull City with more optimism than warranted…
