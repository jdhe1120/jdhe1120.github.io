About

I was born in the US, then moved to Tokyo, Hong Kong, and back to the US, while visiting my grandparents in Shanghai every year. Without realizing it, my upbringing has drawn me to building bridges between worlds. As a TA for five CS courses in undergrad, I loved helping psych and gov majors apply their learnings to their fields. As a PM at Microsoft, I loved being a connector, taking Microsoft Research algorithms into production as a patented anomaly-detection platform and growing it from 0 to 70+ Azure and M365 teams. I recently graduated from HBS, where I led the 450-member AI Club and taught workshops on agentic AI and evals for folks with no technical background.

Lately I've been building CantoLevelUp.com and MandarinLevelUp.com to help heritage speakers like me improve their speaking skills to grow closer to family and friends. When I'm not building, you'll find me on a basketball court, or watching the Clippers and Hull City with more optimism than warranted…
